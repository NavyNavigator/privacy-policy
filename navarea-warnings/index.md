# Privacy Policy for MaritimeHub Maritime Warnings

**Effective date:** July 11, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **MaritimeHub Maritime Warnings** mobile application (“the App”) accesses, uses, stores, and shares information.

The App provides access to NAVAREA, Coastal, Local, and NAVTEX maritime warnings, including map viewing, offline caching, printable reports, and ECDIS user-chart export functions.

**Developer:** Volodymyr Reida

## 2. Information We Collect

The App does not require account registration.

The developer does not intentionally collect the following information through the App:

- names;
- postal addresses;
- phone numbers;
- contacts;
- photographs or videos;
- payment card details;
- advertising identifiers;
- user profiles;
- passwords.

The App does not contain advertising SDKs and does not use personal information for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

## 3. Location Information

The App may request access to the device’s location when the user activates a location-based function such as **“Where am I”**.

Location information is used to:

- display the user’s current position on the maritime warning map;
- show the user’s position relative to NAVAREA and other warning areas;
- update the position marker while the location function is active.

Location access is optional and requires the user’s permission.

The App processes location coordinates locally on the device. The App does not intentionally transmit the user’s location coordinates to the developer’s server.

The App does not create or store a location history.

Location access is used only while the App is in use and while the user has activated the location function.

The user may disable the location function inside the App or revoke location permission at any time through the Android device settings.

The main warning-list functions remain available without location permission.

## 4. Internet Access

The App uses an internet connection to download maritime warning information, update timestamps, warning geometry, and related data.

The App connects to a developer-operated service hosted using Google Cloud infrastructure.

When the App connects to an online service, standard technical information may be processed automatically by the hosting provider, including:

- IP address;
- date and time of the request;
- requested server endpoint;
- response status;
- basic diagnostic and security information.

This technical information may be used only for service operation, security, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and performance monitoring.

It is not used for advertising and is not sold to third parties.

## 5. Maritime Warning Data

Maritime warning information displayed by the App may originate from third-party maritime information providers and official maritime warning sources.

This information relates to maritime safety and navigation and does not contain personal information supplied by the user.

## 6. Local Storage and Offline Cache

The App stores downloaded maritime warning data in the App’s private local storage.

This may include:

- NAVAREA warnings;
- Coastal warnings;
- Local warnings;
- NAVTEX warnings;
- warning coordinates and geometry;
- server update timestamps;
- locally generated map files;
- technical cache files.

This storage allows the App to load warnings faster and provide limited offline access when an internet connection is unavailable.

Cached information remains on the device until:

- it is replaced by updated warning data;
- the user clears the App’s storage through Android settings;
- the Android operating system removes it;
- the App is uninstalled.

The locally cached information is not used for advertising or profiling.

## 7. PDF, XML, and ECDIS Export Files

The App may generate printable reports and ECDIS user-chart files.

Saving or printing these files occurs only after the user selects the relevant function.

The Android system print service or Android document picker may be used to allow the user to select where the file will be printed or saved.

Exported files are stored in the location selected by the user.

The App does not automatically upload exported PDF, XML, or ECDIS files to the developer’s server.

## 8. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Limited technical information may be processed by infrastructure providers only when necessary to:

- deliver maritime warning information;
- operate the online service;
- protect the service from abuse;
- investigate technical errors;
- comply with applicable legal requirements.

The App does not share location coordinates with advertisers or data brokers.

## 9. Third-Party Service Providers

The App uses Google Cloud infrastructure to deliver maritime warning information.

Google Cloud may process limited technical information according to its own privacy and security practices.

The App may also display maritime warning information originating from external maritime data providers. These providers supply navigational warning content and do not receive the user’s device location from the App.

## 10. Data Retention

Locally cached warning information remains on the user’s device until it is updated, cleared, removed by the operating system, or deleted when the App is uninstalled.

Limited server and infrastructure logs may be retained for operational, diagnostic, security, or legal purposes according to the configuration and retention practices of the hosting provider.

The developer does not maintain a database of user accounts or user location histories.

## 11. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the App and its online services.

The App uses secure network connections where supported and stores cached warning information inside the App’s private storage area.

However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 12. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended primarily for maritime professionals, seafarers, navigators, and persons interested in maritime safety information.

The App is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 13. User Choices

Users may:

- deny location permission;
- revoke location permission through Android settings;
- disable the “Where am I” function;
- clear the App’s locally stored data;
- remove exported files from their selected storage location;
- uninstall the App;
- contact the developer with privacy-related questions.

## Maritime Safety Notice

MaritimeHub Maritime Warnings is an informational aid.

The App does not replace official navigational warnings, approved nautical publications, mandatory bridge procedures, ECDIS procedures, Notices to Mariners, NAVTEX receivers, SafetyNET, or other official Maritime Safety Information services.

Users must verify all information using official and approved navigational sources before using it for navigation.
