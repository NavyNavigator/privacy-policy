Report a technical issue

To help us investigate, please include:

  • Your device model and iOS or Android version.
  • Your MaritimeHub app version.
  • The feature involved and the steps to reproduce the issue.
  • An optional screenshot of the error (without personal or sensitive information).

PRO access and purchases

Restore your purchase: Open MaritimeHub and select Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed into the Apple Account or Google Play account used for the purchase and has an internet connection.

Manage or cancel a subscription: On iPhone, use the Apple subscription settings. On Android, open Google Play and go to Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

PRO still not available? Email support and describe the issue. Do not include payment card details or passwords.

Purchases and subscription billing are handled by the relevant app store. Refund requests are reviewed by Apple or Google according to their policies.

About MaritimeHub

MaritimeHub provides calculations, voyage-planning tools, GPS utilities and nautical references designed for seafarers. Many features work offline; certain online services require an internet connection.

MaritimeHub is a supplementary planning and reference tool. Always use official nautical publications, approved navigation equipment and established onboard procedures for operational decisions.
